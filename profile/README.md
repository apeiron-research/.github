## Building artificial worlds to study intelligent behavior.

We investigate how artificial agents learn, reason, adapt, remember, and revise their beliefs when the world proves them wrong — not just whether they can complete a task.

Our central question:

> What happens when an agent's understanding of the world becomes insufficient?

## Current Work

- **[Apeiron](https://github.com/apeiron-research/apeiron)** — VERA, an agent that discovers hidden causal rules in a dynamic environment (VCS) and learns to identify and correct exactly the belief that broke, without forgetting anything else.

## Philosophy

Every project here starts as a controlled experiment, not a product:

- A clear research question and hypothesis
- A minimal environment
- Measurable agent behavior
- Honest failure analysis — we study failures, not hide them

More projects will be added as real research questions emerge from ongoing work.
